# Named threads

Find the threads that matter by their titles, across every channel you have joined.

## Getting started

1. Open **Threads** from the navigation, or press Cmd+Shift+H (Ctrl+Shift+H on Windows and Linux).
2. Threads with a title are listed newest activity first. Choose one to open it beside its channel.
3. Use the channel menu to show one channel's threads, and the **×** beside it to show all of them again.
4. On the desktop app, choose **Pin** to keep a thread at the top, or **Mark done** to move it into the collapsed Done section until it has new activity. Pins and done marks are private to you and follow you across your desktops.
5. Rows show unread replies in threads you follow (choose **Mark read** to clear them) and how long an agent has been working in a thread. The sidebar's Threads entry shows a dot while a named thread is unread, or how many have agents working.
6. Choose **Rename** to edit a title in place: press Enter to save or Escape to cancel. Save it empty to remove the title.

Titles are shared with everyone in the channel. When you start a thread by mentioning agents, the first one you mention names it with its `thread_title` tool; the `buzz` CLI sets them with `buzz threads title set`, and Buzz 0.x shows the same titles. A titled thread also shows its title under its first message in the channel and on its thread tab; renaming a thread tab sets the shared title. The list updates as titles change and replies arrive.
